- **2026-11-08:** art_gate drift basis is declared per asset (T30, Ty's call). anchor_drift (pixel difference vs the anchor's frame 0) is right for frames of one subject and wrong for different subjects in one style: tonight 10/12 monster portraits, most goods and all tiles failed drift at 0.55–0.93 while passing every style check, and regenerations could not converge (candidates clustered within ~0.04).
